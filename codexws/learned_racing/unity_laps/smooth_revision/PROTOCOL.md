# Long-run local steering and pace experiment

Run 80 seeded training layouts under the real Unity motor/contact loop, starting from e8c3533. Each layout receives all seven actions from the same seed, start, camera, controller state and obstacle geometry. The archived V2 policy (file SHA-256 22d6f47258e079bd74a93574010c02f78afb605e38c7fe8191d23a93cc4ea729) always runs in the background and supplies the complete reference. An optional local layer can override the short encounter. Fallen, crashed, missed and non-intervening episodes remain in the dataset.

## Actions and observations

Only causal A4 line and cone observations, recent track motion, measured body speed and yaw, and the current command are available during selection. Prop positions, phases, geometric track curvature, prior lap locations and judging windows are restricted to scenario generation or scoring. The simulation provides body pose for the tracker’s ego-motion compensation. No full map is loaded into the driving policy.

The new actions target the visible lane or the smallest left/right displacement that clears predicted cone occupancy. The body half-width and cone radius account for physical contact. The learned empirical trajectory bank provides extra space and speed alternatives; extra space is capped at 0.5 m. The action can adopt new observations within the same encounter without resetting the side, then reduce its offset through a distance-based recovery. The steering controller still follows the current perceived road heading immediately. Frame association, local forecasts, geometric templates, rate limits and the 7-action abstraction remain human-designed. Which action to select is trained from paired Unity outcomes.

## Objectives

A contact or boundary departure earns a negative terminal return. In successful training episodes the only explicit positive clearance term is `0.16 * clip(minConeM,0,0.5) / 0.5`. A measured 2 m near miss receives the same distance reward as one at 0.5 m. Other terms favor efficient clearing and smooth driving. Smoothness combines total change in body heading relative to track tangent, yaw command variation, body lateral travel and extra path length, all normalized by judged progress distance. Failed truncated episodes receive no credit for quickly terminating. Steady yaw during an ordinary bend does not itself count as excess turning. The geometry used to calculate these returns is unseen by the policy.

Natural cruise uses V2’s original clear-road operating point. The development speed gate requires actual 22 m progress divided by traversal time at or above 4.00 m/s on each of three separately seeded clear straights after 12 m of approach. The distance fraction at or above 4 m/s is reported separately. Lower speeds in bends and during obstacle encounters remain allowed. Only independently registered blind gates support final speed acceptance.

## Splits and policy selection

- Training IDs tr001…tr080 each have arms a0…a6. The first 64 layouts support checkpoints after 32 and 64 contexts. The final 16 add complementary straight/curved configurations and longer natural approach/return windows. Each physical training attempt is counted once. `events.jsonl` and `runs/` retain restart evidence and outcomes.
- Development uses the same 14 obstacle segments, 3 clear straight gates and 2 mixed complete laps for the reference and every actual candidate comparison. Forests at 32, 64 and 80 contexts, plus end-stage data-calibrated safety alternatives, are distinguished from genuine holdouts. Both ordinary grouped validation and action-specific training regressions are retained. The policy-tree alternative forbids selecting an action in a learned leaf if that action introduced a contact on its training examples. The second optional calibrated alternative derives action debits from out-of-fold predicted advantage and measured safety. These methods can still err on new layouts.
- The sealed model prioritizes development acceptance including actual straight pace, successful complete laps, no newly failed layouts and reduced measured heading/yaw command variation. Ranking records every candidate, endpoint and physical run digest. A candidate failing a development or final gate remains explicitly experimental.
- The independent evaluator owns 24 new segment layouts and 8 complete laps. Their numbers stay hidden until the model and best fixed action are frozen. The same solver, seeded body spawn, physical scenario, observation frequency and capture settings are applied to each reference/learned pair. The prespecified constant action is selected first by completed training layouts, then measured steering cost, then encounter time. When that constant action is zero its test scores reuse the actual V2 baselines.
- The optional film replays of training are checked against their original physics and A4/command traces. They are labelled replays, counted outside training and do not enter any fit.
