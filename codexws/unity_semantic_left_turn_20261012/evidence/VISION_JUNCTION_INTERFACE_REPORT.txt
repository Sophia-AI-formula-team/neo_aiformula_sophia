3. Minimal genuine-vision candidate pipeline (diagnostic first)

A. Consume same-sequence binary lane PNG plus the original RGB and calibration.
   Preserve the complete image topology, including current components rejected
   by the driving candidate selector. Use predicted lane pixels, never the Unity
   label/mask camera, island mesh, evaluator route or known junction coordinates.

B. Project only supported ground-plane pixels/rays, retaining explicit visibility
   and invalid-ray masks. Record lane-edge polylines, endpoints and intersections;
   do not reduce every component to y=a+bx or the selected three ABC points.
   Existing VisionLaneFollower/lane_publisher.py:405-425 clips to forward0..10m,
   lateral +/-4m and fits one line. This intentionally loses branch structure;
   the driving projection's crop must not define the full junction observation.

C. Require positive structural evidence: boundary continuation before an opening,
   two plausible mouth edges or a branch-aligned marking/edge, and visible
   continuation of the forward corridor. Use same-frame RGB to distinguish
   supported road/green-edge structure, occlusion and image boundary clipping.
   A gap alone is UNKNOWN: dashed lines, crosswalks, service strips, glare,
   occlusion, failed segmentation and a real branch can all create gaps.
   If no genuine drivable segmentation exists, label the result a lane-boundary
   junction CANDIDATE, not a verified traversable corridor.

D. Keep side/bearing/opening interval with uncertainty, visible support pixels,
   rejection reasons and current capture identity. Confirm using multiple actual
   distinct captures whose independently extracted geometry is consistent after
   capture-time odometry association. Do not paint old boundaries into a new
   mask or call extrapolated geometry currently observed. A historical accepted
   feature may remain a MAP observation with its age/source, not a fresh detector.

E. Associate accepted observations to official graph junctions using ordered
   travel, branch bearing/side, adjacency and accepted localization uncertainty.
   A single forward camera cannot observe all incident roads simultaneously;
   do not claim full node degree from one image. Five similar left-T observations
   alone may leave cyclic ID ambiguity. Preserve ambiguity rejection until real
   asymmetry/additional feature evidence resolves it. No forced nearest-CAD ID.

A separate true drivable model could support an opening/free-space branch, but
adding/training/swapping that model is outside a small wiring change. A hardcoded
RGB pavement-color heuristic is not a recovered A3 drivable output either.

4. Observability and projection limits

- AIFormulaRgbCameraTopic.cs:17-18 uses fx=fy~254.392, cx~330.013, cy~181.150
  at640x360; :66-72 builds this projection. Approximate horizontal FOV103deg,
  with about52deg left and51deg right; vertical FOV about71deg.
- A purely lateral90deg branch cannot be seen when abreast of the camera. To
  see lateral offset L on the left, a forward-facing ray needs forward distance
  roughly >=L/1.297, before considering occlusions. An outer-lane view must look
  farther across the inner lane, so a branch may leave the FOV before entry.
  Observe while approaching and retain it as a localized map landmark; do not
  invent a current mouth after it leaves the image. Camera/lamp stays unchanged.
- Ground rays near the horizon are ill conditioned. An apparent opening at the
  image edge has incomplete support. Projection uncertainty must grow with depth,
  pixel localization error, pitch/roll and calibration/ground-height uncertainty.
- RoadDetector.CameraCalibration():151-163 uses fixed vehicle-relative camera
  R,t. Actual camera rendering at RgbCameraTopic.cs:48-50 follows the vehicle's
  current transform; the existing IPM assumes the base ground plane z=0. Dynamic
  pitch/roll or body-height offsets therefore need measured attitude/ground-plane
  treatment or explicit rejection/uncertainty, not silently exact projections.
- Mask absence outside the FOV or rejected projection is unobserved, not free.
  Existing road-like asphalt also occurs inside islands: lane-only absence cannot
  certify connectivity. This must be resolved visually, not using CAD as input.

5. Capture/odometry envelope needed before MapAssist can consume features

Existing atomic guarantees:
- RgbCameraTopic.cs:47,55 records monotonic CapturedAt and increments Sequence.
- RoadDetector.cs:180-193 records pending sequence/time and sends camera K/R/t,
  epoch and sim_time inside lane_control. The worker echoes sequence,input_sha256.
- RoadDetector.cs:218-237 verifies sequence and exact INPUT PNG hash, then loads
  RGB/masks/overlay together and sets FrameSimulationTime. FrameAge is measured
  since capture, not since successful display.
- SemanticPlanning.cs:106-125 supplies CURRENT tick odometry and, once per new
  detector sequence, vision epoch, capture sim_time, age, points, color and range.
  Current tick pose is not the capture pose of a delayed image.
- semantic_worker.py:310-324 rejects duplicate/out-of-order/stale vision using
  max(monotonic age, current_sim_time-capture_sim_time); :371 onward stamps the
  current odometry with the tick simulation time.

Required additional junction observation contract (proposed, not installed):
- camera_session/epoch, capture_sequence, capture_sim_time, capture_monotonic_time
  or bound age, receive time, input image hash, output mask identity and calibration
  version/hash including K,R,t,width,height, image orientation/distortion policy.
- observations[] with feature ID local to the camera epoch, type candidate/
  confirmed/unknown, side/bearing, mouth/edge geometry in explicitly named camera
  or base frame at CAPTURE, uncertainty/support/visibility and reason. Never an
  evaluator-supplied global junction ID or truth world position.
- capture-time odom bracket with timestamps/frame IDs, position/yaw and covariance,
  plus measured attitude/ground-plane information when used in projection. Pose
  interpolation must reject excessive gaps/reset/discontinuity; record the bracket
  and interpolation weight. Never attach the response-arrival pose to old pixels.
- Existing simulator odom is explicitly ideal Unity body odometry. It may locate
  an independently detected feature downstream, as the current MapAssist already
  uses it; it must NOT label a pixel, set branch geometry or decide that a junction
  exists. Preserve this provenance and distinguish eventual real ROS odometry.
- Camera Capture currently uses transform (possibly render-interpolated), while
  tick odom reads Rigidbody pose. Bound/record that timing difference; synchronization
  requires a deliberate capture contract rather than guessing equivalence.
- Duplicate rejection, fresh age on both clocks, epoch reset/clear, finite-value
  validation and one real frame per observation vote. Odometry-only ticks and
  unsupported lane frames must not duplicate evidence or renew visual freshness.
- A junction observation may be valid when selected ABC is unsupported. Transport
  its independent status without treating it as lane-control permission. Do not
  change LYA ownership, the speed gate, point TTL or current missing-lane stop just
  to collect a junction candidate. Map association and actuation remain separate.

Exact-byte input_sha256 checks the original transport PNG. The harness re-encodes
the accepted Texture2D when saving rgb_*.png; the saved PNG bytes may differ despite
identical decoded pixels. For archive pairing use sequence/capture metadata and
decoded-pixel hashes, or preserve the original request PNG bytes explicitly.

Conclusion: current data supports a genuine lane-boundary junction diagnostic,
not an already available drivable-opening detector. Start with offline candidate
evidence and false-positive/observability review; extend the observation protocol
only after that evidence. Do not replace the official matcher with unobserved
apex/junction positions or promise automatic node correspondence from one camera.
