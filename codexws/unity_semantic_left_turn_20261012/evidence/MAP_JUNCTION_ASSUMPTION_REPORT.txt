4. Algorithm mismatch, independently of map scale

route_fusion.py:84-137 treats every official outer node as an observed turn.
- :95-100 uses the angle between incident official CHORDS; nearly straight
  nodes(<8deg) are rejected outright as node_has_no_observable_turn.
- :101-114 seeds by normalized chord progress, searches only +/-4m by default,
  and measures the turn of recorded points at s-2m,s,s+2m.
- :115 requires the same sign and at least max(.10rad, .25*expected).
- :128-136 requires all five inferred turn locations to occur separately/orderly.

Official chord angles and minimum local measured turn:
node00:85.219239deg -> >=21.304810deg
node01:79.804067deg -> >=19.951017deg
node02:99.095002deg -> >=24.773751deg
node03:49.628996deg -> >=12.407249deg
node04:46.252695deg -> >=11.563174deg

Two distinct contract problems follow:
A. Junction position is not a bend observation. A correct outer-lap traversal of
   an actual straight T junction supplies no required signed corner at that node.
B. Feature scale differs. For a constant-radius arc R, the three-point chord
   turn with +/-2m arc-length samples is approximately2/R radians, not the total
   bend angle. At R15..23m it is only about5..8deg. Thus even a correctly located
   broad bend may fail those11.6..24.8deg minimums. Jagged tracking could create
   an accidental stronger feature; that would not establish correct junction ID.

The failure is not repaired by better scale, more laps on the same geometry,
relaxing the RMSE/turn threshold, enlarging search until it finds another corner,
or pretending unavailable alignment parameters are an accepted localization.
route_fusion.py:225-228 itself says consumers must use accepted final node
positions/progress, not the retained similarity's original-node coordinates.

5. Smallest coherent repair candidates for a decision

A. Explicit simulator map input, with separate geometry and decision roles.
   Preserve the official file. Add a versioned simulator-specific input only
   after approval, with declared coordinate frame/provenance/metric assumptions.
   Distinguish bend landmarks (used for shape/phase registration) from traffic
   junction IDs (used for connectivity/entry/blocked-edge decisions). Curved edge
   geometry or tangent metadata must prevent junction-to-junction chords from
   inventing turn features. The genuine odometry lap remains the recorded route;
   map-based junction positions must be labeled prior-map-derived, not learned.

   Config-only coordinate replacement is insufficient today: accurate collinear
   north junctions trigger node_has_no_observable_turn. Selecting only four bend
   nodes via route_node_ids is also insufficient: map_assist.py:688-707 considers
   only that outer list as detour entrances and could start at bends instead of
   actual branch junctions. Separate landmark matching from junction eligibility.

   Straight-junction association requires either:
   - accepted, unambiguous registration from independent geometric landmarks,
     metric-prior branch position and known scale with bounded uncertainty,
     unique projection onto the correct trusted outer segment, tangent/branch
     side/order agreement, and separation from neighbouring junctions; OR
   - an independently observed junction feature as in B below.
   If metric prior lengths are explicitly disallowed, normalized lap fraction
   between remote bends cannot recover an unobserved along-straight junction.
   Report association unavailable instead of silently treating that fraction as
   a learned node. Preserving odometry route scale must remain explicit.

B. Genuine junction observations.
   Add timestamped, calibrated branch-mouth/crossroad observations from current
   RGB/lane mask, with branch side/bearing/opening evidence, tracking consistency
   and uncertainty. Associate in odometry with graph degree, cyclic order and
   surrounding accepted landmarks; require repeated observations and reject
   ambiguous service strips, crosswalks or temporarily missing lane fragments.
   Today's selected ABC line alone is not such a feature. A red color alone has
   neither junction ID nor branch geometry. Do not promote evaluator CAD mouths
   or delayed historic projected points into visual detections.

For both candidates retain normal unavailable behavior: real perception may
continue, but no mapped detour while fusion/junction identity is untrusted.
map_assist.py:680-708 requires available fusion, mapped topology, matched fresh
odom and actual entry proximity. Do not remove these gates to demonstrate a turn.
Also preserve LYA, plant, camera, lamp pose, physical motion and all failed runs.
