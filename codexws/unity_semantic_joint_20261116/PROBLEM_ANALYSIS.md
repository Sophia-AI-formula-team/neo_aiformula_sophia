Final panel-only mode is a simulator-specific, default-off adapter. Missing RGB configuration is rejected; invalid/missing calibration yields unknown; no model-color fallback or old-color replay is introduced. Existing calibrated panel tests, two-frame confirmation, native traffic voting and TTL remain. Physical lamp truth is evaluation-only, and no signal distance is passed into semantic. The default mode still matches the original pre-v1 helper on all 3268 saved frames. JPEG replay is not original PNG inference or independent hardware acceptance.

The rejected four-point candidate remains local at `TRIAL/audit/rejected_short_side_candidate/`; small receipts are in `evidence/rejected_short_side/`. Neither its upstream_adapter change nor its enabling C# belongs to the deployment manifest. Finite commands and default-off parity did not justify its artificial curvature. No alternative indices/interpolation/gain changes were developed after rejection.

Automatic topology matching remains unresolved. A low best RMSE cannot erase a legal near-cost alternative, and first-lap outside-boundary odometry cannot alone prove observation of interior roads. The early red stop/entry-radius reachability risk is separate from current fusion rejection. Both require explicit future analysis before a versioned source change; neither was hidden by the final successful green-recovery laps.
